BLUE CHAIN AQUA - VOICE AI TEST

1. Set GROQ_API_KEY in your local server/deployment environment. Do not put the key in config.js.
2. Save config.js.
3. Double-click index.html and open it in Chrome.
4. Open Ask Aqua AI.
5. Choose the answer language:
   - English
   - తెలుగు
   - हिन्दी
6. Press the microphone button and allow microphone permission.
7. Speak in English, Telugu, Hindi, or a mixture.
8. The assistant transcribes what you said and answers ONLY in your selected language.

Examples:
- Select Telugu. Speak in English: "How do I maintain shrimp pond water quality?"
  Expected: answer in Telugu.
- Select Hindi. Speak in Telugu.
  Expected: answer in Hindi.
- Select English. Speak in Hindi.
  Expected: answer in English.

Voice output:
- Voice On is enabled by default.
- The browser will read the answer in the selected language when a matching Chrome/Windows voice is available.
- Use Voice Off if you only want text.
- A speaker button also appears beside each AI answer so you can replay it.

Note: Groq Whisper is used for multilingual speech-to-text. The browser's built-in speech synthesis is used for spoken answers because Groq's current hosted TTS models support English and Arabic, not Telugu/Hindi.

MOBILE VOICE OUTPUT UPDATE
- Answers are split into short speech chunks so Android Chrome does not truncate long replies.
- Markdown/URLs are cleaned before speech.
- Telugu/Hindi voice selection prefers a matching installed voice.
- Test on HTTPS, not file://.
